Things I Enjoy

When I am not working on school assignments, I like spending time on activities that help me relax, stay active, and have fun. Sports and being outdoors are some of the biggest interests in my life.

  • Playing football
  • Watching and following football
  • Watching and playing soccer
  • Hiking and spending time outdoors
  • Learning about computers and technology
  • Listening to music and hanging out with friends

Why I Enjoy These Hobbies

Football is especially important to me because I have been playing it for a long time. It has become a big part of who I am and is something I have grown up with. Playing football has taught me about teamwork, commitment, discipline, and working toward a goal with other people.

I also enjoy soccer, hiking, and spending time outdoors because they give me a chance to stay active and have fun. Technology is another interest of mine because there is always something new to learn and explore.
